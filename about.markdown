# About

At Acelen, I lead the industrial AI roadmap and Acel-IA, a generative AI platform that connects refinery data with engineering documents. My teams build predictive models for soft sensors, maintenance, forecasting and catalyst monitoring.

I started in bioinformatics and bioenergy research, working with plant genomes and scientific databases. I then applied machine learning to insurance and energy before moving to refineries. Refinery data isn’t that different from a genome: dense, context-dependent and unforgiving when misread.
